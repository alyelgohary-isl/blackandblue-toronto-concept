Black+Blue/Tomahawk Steak

What is a tomahawk steak?

A tomahawk is a thick bone-in rib eye cut with the full rib bone left long and cleaned, so it looks like an axe; at Black+Blue the Prime Tomahawk is a butcher cut at market price, with daily availability from your server; Vancouver also offers an Australian Wagyu Tomahawk.

It is a rib eye, the richest of the classic steaks, left on a rib bone that has been frenched (scraped clean) to its full length. Most weigh well over two pounds and are cooked slowly, rested and carved for the table.

The bone does not add much flavour on its own, but cooking on the bone keeps the meat beside it juicy, and the size makes it a shared centrepiece.

Questions

How many people does a tomahawk serve?

Usually two to three, with sides.

Is a tomahawk always available?

It is a butcher cut: ask your server for the day’s availability, or mention it when you book.

What sauces go with it?

Béarnaise, truffle jus, red wine jus or peppercorn, 5 each, or the Trio of Sauces, 15.
