What is prime rib?

Prime rib is a standing rib roast of beef, slow-roasted on the bone and carved into thick slices, traditionally with jus and Yorkshire pudding; at Black+Blue a 14oz cut is 78 in Toronto and 73 in Vancouver, with three-course prime rib dinners every Sunday in both.

The name comes from the rib primal, the section of the animal the roast is cut from. It is roasted slowly and whole so the centre stays evenly pink from edge to edge, then rested and sliced to order.

The classic accompaniments are English: the roasting juices served as jus, horseradish, and a tall Yorkshire pudding made in the hot drippings.

Questions

Is prime rib the same as rib eye?

They come from the same section. Prime rib is roasted whole and sliced; a rib eye is cut first and grilled as a steak.

When is Prime Rib Sunday?

Every Sunday from 3:00pm.

Is prime rib always Prime grade?

Not necessarily: “prime” refers to the rib primal. Black+Blue’s Sunday prime rib is 10 oz Northern Gold.
