# Black+Blue Toronto — creative direction

1. **True of this restaurant only:** Canada's Kobe-Beef certified steakhouse, dry-aging its cuts for 28–45 days in a Himalayan salt-brick locker displayed in the centre of the dining room, in the old Toronto Stock Exchange at 130 King St West.
2. **Who's eating:** Financial District expense accounts (Lunch 45, client dinners, holiday parties), occasion diners from the 905 booking a Wellington or a tomahawk, groups up to 350. They also look at watch catalogues, auction houses, hotel sites.
3. **Analogue:** the evening-sale auction catalogue (Sotheby's / Christie's lot pages): one object per plate on a dark ground, lot number, provenance line, grade, estimate; hairline rules, small caps, huge margins. The old stock exchange was a trading floor; rare cuts sold by the ounce are lots.
4. **Idea:** "A Superior Selection, lot by lot." (Their own page title.)
5. **Brand and words:** gold monogram + Didone wordmark, their navy #1C2236, gold #987C44/#AA844D, cream #EBE5DD; their Helvetica Neue for text. All home and inner copy theirs, verbatim.

| Axis | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | one deep navy #121726 (the "Blue"), cream ink, gold hairlines | the brand's own blue, the step-and-repeat colour; their photography is low-key and sits like objects on velvet |
| Type | Bodoni Moda (display, echoes the wordmark) + Helvetica Neue/Inter stack (theirs) + Geist Mono for lot specs only | catalogue: Didone titles, grotesk text, tabular specs |
| Script | none | the client doesn't use one |
| Scale/rhythm | small type with huge margins; one large Didone line per screen | an evening-sale catalogue |
| Grid | centred plate, 12-col with a visible spec line beneath (Lot · Origin · Grade/Aged · Price) | a catalogue page |
| Hero | the plate (salt locker still) with the copy *beneath*: H1 left, their buttons right | letterboxed film with copy under it |
| Film framing | letterboxed plate ≈ 2.2:1 centred, gold hairline border, caption beneath; phones: 4:5 plate | catalogue plate |
| Beat copy | "Lot 02" + their dish name + their menu line + price in mono | the lot entry |
| Home sections | film → the week (their weekly features as a Mon–Sun calendar of sales) → private dining (their copy) → distinctions line → visit (hours, address, parking) | everything on their current home, ordered by how a guest decides |
| Components | square corners, hairline rules, text links with arrows, a 7-column calendar, numbered lots | catalogue |
| Images | crisp, matted inside a hairline frame, never full-bleed after the film | prints in a catalogue |
| Motion signature | the plate's gold border draws itself around the plate at each cut (perimeter trace), nothing else | one signature |

Alternates considered: (a) "The trading floor" — a stock-ticker of today's cuts and per-oz prices across a black ground (too gimmicky for a luxury room); (b) "Black and blue" — the steak term: a seared-black/rare-blue two-ground split hero (strong but AB-banding risk).
