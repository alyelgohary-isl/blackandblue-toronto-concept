Black+Blue/Seafood Tower

What is on a seafood tower?

A seafood tower is a tiered platter of chilled shellfish served on ice; at Black+Blue Toronto the Seafood Tower (250) has east coast oysters, colossal shrimp, Dungeness crab, Atlantic lobster, king crab and marinated mussels and clams; Vancouver’s Chilled Seafood Platter is 275.

Towers come from the French plateau de fruits de mer: everything cold, cracked and ready to eat, stacked so a table can share it as the first course of a steakhouse dinner.

The usual sauces are cocktail sauce, a mignonette of shallots and vinegar for the oysters, fresh horseradish and lemon.

At Black+Blue

Seafood Tower: ½ dozen east coast oysters, colossal shrimp, ½ dungeness crab, ½ atlantic lobster, king crab, marinated mussels & clams, cocktail sauce, marie rose, mignonette, horseradish. The Deluxe Seafood Tower adds a dozen oysters, a whole crab and lobster, a king salmon roll and tuna, hamachi and scallop nigiri.

Questions

How many people does a seafood tower serve?

The Seafood Tower is a generous starter for a table to share; the Deluxe is built for a larger group.

Is there caviar?

Yes: Royal Osetra (168), Imperial Osetra (174) and Beluga (450), 30 grams, with blini and traditional garnish.

When are oysters $2?

At Happy Hour, daily from 2:30pm to 5:30pm.
